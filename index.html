BLACKJACK

Get a hand value as close to 21 as possible without going over.

Card values:
- Number cards (2-10) are worth their face value.
- Face cards (J, Q, K) are worth 10.
- Aces can be worth 1 or 11, whichever benefits the hand.

Player actions:
- Hit: Ask for another card.
- Stand: Keep your current hand.

Dealer's turn:
- The dealer must keep drawing cards until reaching at least 17.
- If the dealer goes over 21, players win automatically if they haven't busted.

Winning or losing:
- You win if your hand is closer to 21 than the dealer's.
- You lose if you go over 21.
- If the dealer's hand is closer to 21, you lose.
